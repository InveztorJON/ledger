Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short: Ledger reads your bank statement on your own phone or computer. The file is never uploaded, and Ledger's servers do not store your transactions. If you use the AI coach, a short summary of your numbers is sent to generate an answer and is not kept by Ledger.

Who we are

Ledger is a product of Jonspire Limited ("we"), a private company limited by shares incorporated in Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 and registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (RC 9187600), with its business address in Lagos, Nigeria. For privacy questions or to use your rights, contact jonspirelimited@gmail.com.

What Ledger processes and where

  • Bank statements you choose to upload. Read on your device to find dates, descriptions and amounts. The file itself is discarded after reading. If you use a password to open a PDF, it is used once and never saved.
  • Transactions, tags, check-ins, quiz answers and progress. Stored in your browser on your device, only if you switch on "Remember my data on this device". Otherwise they are cleared when you close Ledger.
  • AI coach questions (optional). When you ask the coach a question, Ledger sends your question, the recent conversation and a short summary of your numbers (monthly totals, categories and patterns) to our coach service, which asks Anthropic's Claude model for an answer. It never sends your statement, individual transactions or the names of people you pay. Our coach service does not store or log the content of your questions. Anthropic processes the request to generate the answer under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

Why we process it (legal basis)

We rely on your consent, which you give in the app before your first statement is read and separately before using the AI coach. You can withdraw consent at any time in Ledger under Me → Privacy & data.

Services we use

  • Netlify hosts the app and runs the coach service. Like most hosts, it records technical request information such as IP address and browser type.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model for the optional coach.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare (cdnjs) deliver fonts and a spreadsheet-reading library. They receive standard request information but never your financial data.

Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria. Before any data leaves your device for these providers, Ledger tells you and asks for your consent (for the AI coach, through its own switch). These providers use contractual and technical safeguards, including encryption in transit, and you can use Ledger fully without the AI coach.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can access, correct, delete and move your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Because your data stays on your device, you can do most of this yourself in Ledger: download everything (Me → Download my data), remove a statement, or delete everything. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

How long we keep data

Ledger's servers keep no statement data or coach conversations. Data on your device stays until you remove it, switch off "Remember my data", or clear your browser storage.

Children

Ledger is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS with a strict content security policy, runs statement reading on your device, and holds the AI key only on the server. No system is perfectly secure; keep your device locked and your browser up to date.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and show a notice in the app for important changes.
